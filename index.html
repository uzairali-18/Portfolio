How I Help Websites Rank

My SEO services focus on practical search engine optimization that improves visibility without gimmicks. As a freelance SEO specialist, my approach is simple: understand what people search for, structure content properly, and make sure Google can read and rank the site. Every project gets a clear SEO audit, keyword plan, and measurable work — whether you're a small business in Lahore or targeting customers worldwide.

1. Keyword Research

I find keywords people actually search for. Not vanity terms. I look at search intent, competition level, and what makes sense for the site's content. Tools I use: Google Keyword Planner, Ubersuggest, and manual SERP analysis.

2. On-Page SEO

I optimize the parts of the page Google reads: title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure (H1, H2, H3), and internal linking. I make sure the content answers the search query clearly and that the structure makes sense for both users and crawlers.

3. Content Optimization

I write and optimize content based on keyword research and search intent. The goal is to match what users are looking for, not just stuff keywords. I keep it natural, structured, and useful.

4. Basic Technical SEO

I handle the technical basics: mobile responsiveness, page speed optimization, clean HTML structure, proper use of semantic tags, and making sure the site is crawlable. I also set up Google Search Console and submit sitemaps.

Frequently Asked Questions – SEO Services

What does an SEO specialist do?

An SEO specialist improves a website's visibility in Google and other search engines. In my work that means keyword research to find terms people actually search for, on-page SEO (title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal linking), content optimization matched to search intent, and technical basics like crawlability, mobile responsiveness, and page speed.

How much does SEO cost in Pakistan?

In Pakistan, freelance SEO specialists typically charge PKR 20,000 to PKR 50,000 per month depending on scope and competition, while agencies charge PKR 50,000+. As a freelance SEO specialist, I offer flexible, affordable project-based and monthly pricing designed for small businesses and personal websites.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term investment. Most websites start seeing meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months, depending on competition, the site's current state, and how consistently the work is done. Anyone promising overnight #1 rankings is a red flag.

Which SEO tools does Uzair Ali use?

I use Google Keyword Planner and Ubersuggest for keyword research, Google Search Console for indexing and performance monitoring, and manual SERP analysis to understand search intent and competition. I keep the toolset practical and results-focused.

Do you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No honest SEO specialist can guarantee a #1 ranking — Google's algorithm is outside anyone's control. What I guarantee is clean, structured, white-hat SEO work: proper keyword targeting, optimized on-page elements, quality content, and a technically sound website. That is what builds sustainable rankings.
